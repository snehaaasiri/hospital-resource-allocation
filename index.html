Algorithmic Engine

Greedy Allocation Engine

Priority-sorted atomic resource matching with comprehensive decision logs

How the Greedy Algorithm Operates

1. Sort Queue: Patients are strictly ordered by Emergency Priority (Critical 4 > Serious 3 > Moderate 2 > Normal 1). Earliest arrival timestamp is the FIFO tie-breaker.
2. Atomic Matching: Simultaneously checks if an available matching Bed Type, Doctor Specialization, and Medical Equipment exist.
3. Decision: If all 3 match, resources are locked atomically. If any resource is unavailable, the patient transitions to the Waiting List with an explicit clinical bottleneck explanation.
